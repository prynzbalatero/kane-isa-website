  No one is coming to save you!

 The illusion of safety is perpetuated  by the system of our current society. Those who support its structure are bound to keep its rules in place. We all have a natural right to our individual sovereignty: That sovereignty comes at a price. Are you willing to wake up in the dead of night to check your back yard? How well do you know your neighbors? If someone took your child from your hands could you give chase?

   “Call 911!” That statement alone already says you are in a defensive posture. You have to wait for “back up”. What if “back up” never arrives? What if there is no safety net? How do you handle the situation at hand? Get a gun, some dogs, cameras, and some guards. Maybe if you get a castle, you could sleep soundly. Maybe you can bend the environment around you to suit your needs. Maybe you can keep the danger of strangers away if you hire someone to deal with them.

   How much should you pay for your safety? How do you know that you got “your money’s worth”? Did you hire the right people? Maybe the city, or the county, will pick up the slack. After all you paid your taxes; you should be entailed to some sense of safety. Right? The truth is all of us are on our own. “Expect to self rescue” is a humorous slogan within the EMS realm to signify the reality of any immediate emergency situation. “You are on your own”.

  Cameras, municipal police, or guards can't give you some peace then what will? We all have the ability to legally and physically protect ourselves according to both constitution and natural law. Human beings of old have protected themselves for thousands of years before the restrictions of society. 

   ISA is not trying to reinvent the wheel. We recognize that people need to feel a sense of safety, empowerment, and sovereignty. We want you to know that you can protect yourself. Let us advise you on the “how”; You should focus on the “why”.  We here at ISA would prefer that everyone we interact with, ends up feeling strong enough, that they would not want our services.
